## Program description

With your express opt-in, HK Wholesale may send transactional text messages related to your inquiry, order, or delivery. Messages are not promotional. Submitting a general contact inquiry does not enroll you in text messages, and consent to receive messages is not a condition of purchase.

## Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your inquiry, order, or delivery. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan.

## Opt out and help

You may opt out by replying **STOP**. For assistance, reply **HELP** or contact HK Wholesale at [hkwholesalenj@gmail.com](mailto:hkwholesalenj@gmail.com) or +1 (732) 306-5096. These text commands will be available when the messaging program is active.
