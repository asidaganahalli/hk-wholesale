## Program

HK Wholesale's SMS program sends customer-service replies about an inquiry or quote request submitted through our contact form. You must provide your phone number and check the SMS consent box before we use text messages to respond. We do not use this consent for promotional messages.

## Message frequency

You may receive up to 3 messages per inquiry. Actual frequency depends on the inquiry and your response.

## Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan. Carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Opt out and help

Reply **STOP** to opt out of further texts. Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact HK Wholesale at [+1 (732) 306-5096](tel:+17323065096) or [hkwholesalenj@gmail.com](mailto:hkwholesalenj@gmail.com).

## Consent

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or service. For details about information collected and how it is used, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
