## Program

If you provide your phone number and select the optional SMS consent checkbox on the contact form, HK Wholesale may send customer-service replies about that specific inquiry. This consent is not used for marketing or promotional messages. Consent is not a condition of purchase or service.

## Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies, up to 3 messages per inquiry. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan. Carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Opt out and help

Reply **STOP** to opt out of further texts. Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact HK Wholesale at [+1 (732) 306-5096](tel:+17323065096) or [hkwholesalenj@gmail.com](mailto:hkwholesalenj@gmail.com).
