HK Wholesale uses information submitted through this website to respond to questions and service requests.

## Information from the contact form

The contact form may collect your name, email address, phone number, subject, and message. Providing a phone number is optional. If you choose the optional SMS consent checkbox, HK Wholesale may use your number to send customer-service text messages about that specific inquiry.

## Text messages

SMS consent is optional and is not used for marketing or promotional messages. Message frequency varies, up to 3 messages per inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out by replying **STOP** or request help by replying **HELP**.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information on our behalf to operate the website and communications.

## Retention and choices

We retain inquiry and consent records as needed to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal requirements. To ask about information submitted through the site, contact us using the details below.
