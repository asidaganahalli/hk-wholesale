HK Wholesale respects your privacy. This policy describes information submitted through this website and how it may be used.

## Information you provide

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, and the details of your inquiry. This website's contact form does not currently enroll visitors in text messages or collect a phone number for SMS updates.

If you separately provide a mobile number and opt in to transactional text messages through an enrollment form, we may use that number to send messages about your inquiry, order, or delivery, consistent with the consent you gave.

## How information is used

We use information you provide to respond to inquiries and, where you have separately opted in, to provide transactional updates related to your inquiry, order, or delivery. We may use service providers, including LeadConnector, to operate website communications. Those providers process information on our behalf to provide their services.

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

## Text message choices

Text message updates are not currently available through this website. If an SMS enrollment option is introduced, it will be separate and optional. You may opt out of messages by replying STOP and request help by replying HELP, where supported by the program. Message and data rates may apply.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact HK Wholesale at [hkwholesalenj@gmail.com](mailto:hkwholesalenj@gmail.com) or call +1 (732) 306-5096.

## Policy updates

We may update this policy as our website and services change. The current version will be posted on this page.
