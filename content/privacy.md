HK Wholesale uses information submitted through this website to respond to questions and service requests.

## Information from the contact form

The contact form may collect your name, email address, phone number, subject, and message. Submissions are sent to HK Wholesale so we can respond to your inquiry. Providing a phone number is optional unless you choose SMS contact.

## Text messages

If you check the SMS consent box, HK Wholesale may use your phone number to send up to 3 customer-service text messages about that specific inquiry. SMS consent is not used for marketing. You can opt out by replying **STOP** or request help by replying **HELP**.

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information on our behalf only to deliver and support the website and communications; they may not use SMS consent for their own marketing.

## Retention and choices

We retain inquiry and consent records only as needed to respond, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal requirements. To ask about or request access to information submitted through the site, contact us using the details below.
